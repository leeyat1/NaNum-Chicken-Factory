Our Story

NaNum Chicken Factory serves up hand-breaded fried chicken, wings, and tenders right here in Douglasville. From classic wing flavors like hot honey garlic and lemon pepper to hearty Philly cheesesteaks, gyros, and burgers, we've got something for every craving — late into the night.

Every order is made fresh to order, whether you're stopping in, calling ahead, or ordering online for pickup or delivery.
